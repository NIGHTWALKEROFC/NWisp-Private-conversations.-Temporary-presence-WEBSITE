How presence works here

Three ideas the whole app is built around.

Your username is the whole identity

Sign up with an email or a phone number, but neither ever appears on your profile. People see a username and whatever bio you choose — nothing they didn't get from you directly.

Encrypted before it leaves your phone

Messages are encrypted on the sending device and decrypted on the receiving one, using established protocols rather than anything homemade. The server's job is to relay bytes, not read them.

The server has a short memory

Delivered messages are cleared from the server within 24 hours — undelivered ones too. Stories expire on the same clock. What's on your own device is yours to keep or delete.

What's actually in it

Chat and Stories, done properly. Nothing else, on purpose.

Chat

  • 1:1 and small group messaging
  • Text, images, files, video up to 20 MB
  • Replies and reactions
  • Delivery and read status, with a switch to turn it off
  • Per-chat auto-delete timers

Stories

  • Photos and short videos, gone in 24 hours
  • Reply to a Story straight into chat
  • Reactions, no public like counts
  • Pick exactly who can see each Story
  • Custom circles — Friends, Family, Work, whatever you name

Privacy controls

  • Choose who can message you or add you to groups
  • Hide online status and last-seen independently
  • Decide if your number can be used to find you
  • Email and phone stay private by default, always

Security

  • App-open PIN, biometric lock where supported
  • Device and session management, remote logout
  • Login alerts for new devices
  • Local data protected by the device's secure storage

Built honestly, not oversold

What's deliberately not here yet.

No feed. No permanent posts. No reels or livestreaming. No voice or video calls in this version. No ads, no premium tier, no paid anything — v1 is free with nothing behind a paywall, because the goal right now is a small, reliable, private app before it's a big one.

Right now

Where the build actually stands.

NWisp is being built in the open, by one developer, entirely from a phone — no laptop, no dev machine. Core chat and Stories work end to end. What's left before a real release is mostly the unglamorous stuff: notification delivery, tightening the server-side rules, and phone-number sign-in. There's no fixed release date yet, and that's said here on purpose instead of promising one.
